It’s easy to focus solely on what we eat or how much we work out, but I realized that managing the quiet drains on our energy—like endless scrolling—is just as important. I wanted a single, straightforward place to track the three daily essentials.

    Hydration: Tracking water intake to keep energy levels stable throughout the day.

    Nutrition: Logging calories to make sure you are fueling your body properly, without overcomplicating the math.

    Screen Time: Monitoring digital consumption as a reminder to unplug, protect your focus, and reclaim your time offline.
